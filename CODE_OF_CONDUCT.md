# Code of Conduct

TSI DPDP CMS is open to everyone, and we want participation in it to be free of harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualized language or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, insults, or sustained unwelcome contact

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, discussions, and email exchanges with the maintainers, including contribution submissions and security reports.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report violations to **admin@tsicoop.org**. Reports are kept confidential, and we aim to respond within 7 days.

Maintainers may warn, reject contributions from, or block anyone who violates this code of conduct.
