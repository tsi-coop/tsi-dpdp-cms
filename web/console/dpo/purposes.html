Purpose Lifecycle

When a processing purpose ends, close it. Nobody can then give new consent for it, and everyone who had consented to it has that consent withdrawn (and is told). The usual retention period for the purpose then runs, after which processors are asked to erase or de-identify the data, as you choose. Reopening a purpose allows new consent again; it does not bring old consent back.
