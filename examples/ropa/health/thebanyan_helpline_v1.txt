DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROPA requirements for The Banyan's mental health helpline. Covers
support with consented referral and follow-up, emergency response when
life is at risk, and reports about people in distress (third-party
data). Consent is captured once per call by IVR key press. Callers may
stay pseudonymous.

When someone calls, the IVR plays a short notice in their language and
asks for one key press: 
1 for their own support (including referral and follow-up if they ask)
2 to report someone in need. 

That single press is recorded as the consent, with a timestamp and language.
If the counsellor judges there's an immediate risk to life, they can 
escalate to emergency services under legitimate use, with no consent needed
and a log entry for each disclosure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

access, masked mobile numbers, full access logs, no disclosure to
family or employers except to prevent a threat to life.

DPO REVIEW NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. IVR captures one consent per call (press 1 support, press 2
     reporting someone); store the consent artefact with timestamp and
     language. Referral is offered by the counsellor only on request.
  2. Emergency: the counsellor first asks the caller to confirm sharing
     with 112 or a responder and logs the confirmation as consent. If the
     caller cannot or does not respond and life is at risk, disclose
     under Section 7. Log each disclosure with time, recipient, basis
     (consent or Section 7) and reason for audit.
  2a. No purpose is mandatory for the service; declining any of them must
     not stop the call.
  3. Reported persons are not on the call and cannot consent; collect
     only location and condition, and move them into thebanyan_patient_v1
     (with its own consent) if they enter care.
  4. Callers under 18: confirm with counsel whether the health-service
     exemption from guardian consent applies.
  5. Calls are not recorded. Mask mobile numbers on dashboards; purge jobs
     must cover AWS backups and Exotel call logs.
