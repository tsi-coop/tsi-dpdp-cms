DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROPA requirements for a standard corporate website's visitor consent
policy. Covers essential site operation and security, contact-form

measurement. Intended as a starting template: replace the illustrative
processors, retention periods and contacts with your organisation's own.
